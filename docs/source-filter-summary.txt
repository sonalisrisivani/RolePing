ROLEPING — SIMPLE SOURCE AND FILTER SUMMARY
As of: 2 October 2026

TOTAL JOB SOURCES USING FOR NOW: 1

Names of the sources:
1. Greenhouse (Razorpay jobs only)

Important:
- Greenhouse is the platform. Razorpay is the employer we fetch from it.
- one-job.json is one sample job from Greenhouse, NOT another site.
- greenhouse-response.json contains the full response from that same source.


FIELDS IN THE PROFILE SECTION

1. Role — specific roles or Any
2. Location — specific locations or Any
3. Your years of experience
4. Skills — specific skills or Any
5. Role type — Full-time, Internship or Any
6. Work mode — Remote, Hybrid, On-site or Any
7. Minimum salary
8. Salary currency
9. Salary period — year, month or hour
10. What to do when salary is unknown
11. What to do when eligibility is unconfirmed

Other profile fields, NOT job filters:
- Your name
- Daily digest time
- Timezone
- Pause notifications (delivery is not active yet)


SOURCE COMPARED WITH PROFILE FIELDS

GREENHOUSE (RAZORPAY ONLY)

1. Role: YES — compared with the job title.
   Any skips the role filter. Synonyms and descriptions are not checked yet.

2. Location: YES — compared with the main job location text.
   Any skips the location filter. City aliases and conflicting locations
   are not resolved yet.

3. Your years of experience: NO — saved only.
   The response has no dedicated experience field. Description extraction
   is not implemented yet.

4. Skills: NO — saved only, including the Any choice.
   The response has no dedicated skills list. Description extraction
   is not implemented yet.

5. Role type: NO — saved only.
   Full-time versus internship is not verified from the source.

6. Work mode: NO — saved only.
   Remote, hybrid and on-site requirements are not verified.

7. Minimum salary: NO — saved only.
   There is no dedicated salary field in our sample, and we do not yet
   extract salary from descriptions.

8. Salary currency: NO — no extracted job salary to compare.

9. Salary period: NO — no extracted job salary to compare.

10. Unknown salary choice: WORKS AS A POLICY, not a salary comparison.
    Show with a caveat = allow jobs with unknown salary.
    Exclude = remove jobs with unknown salary.
    All current jobs are treated as salary unknown.

11. Unconfirmed eligibility choice: WORKS AS A POLICY, not verification.
    Show with a caveat = allow jobs with unconfirmed eligibility.
    Confirmed only = remove all current jobs because we do not yet
    verify their eligibility requirements.

These rules apply to Matching jobs. All jobs does not use profile filters.
Both tabs hide closed jobs and jobs from disabled sources.


EXTRA SOURCE FIELDS AVAILABLE FOR POSSIBLE FUTURE FILTERS

Available but NOT used as profile filters yet:
1. Company name
2. Department (e.g. Engineering, Finance, Sales)
3. Business unit / office label (not necessarily a physical location)
4. First publication date
5. Last source update date
6. Posting language (not a language proficiency requirement)
7. Extra location metadata (can conflict with the main location)
8. Job description (possible evidence for skills, experience and salary)

Present as a key but empty in all 21 sampled jobs:
- Application deadline

Missing as dedicated fields in this sample:
- Salary amount, currency and pay period
- Required years of experience
- Required skills
- Verified work mode and usable employment type
- Degree, graduation batch and work-authorization requirements

Some missing details may be written in job descriptions. We have not
implemented extraction, so they must not be treated as verified filters.
This describes our Razorpay sample, not every employer using Greenhouse.


WHAT REALLY FILTERS JOBS TODAY?

Role + location + your choices about unknown salary/eligibility.

Salary amounts, experience, skills, role type and work mode are saved,
but are NOT compared yet. They should be clearly labelled as inactive
filters until comparison is implemented.
